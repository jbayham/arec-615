From optimization to roots

These algorithms solve f(x) = 0. To find a stationary point of an objective, enter its first derivative (the FOC). Then check the second-order condition separately.

What the methods assume

Bisection needs a continuous function and opposite signs at the bracket endpoints. Newton needs a derivative and a suitable starting guess; it can cycle or diverge.
